Statutory Policies & Legal Protocols

Practicing under the NMC Telemedicine Guidelines (2020) & Mental Healthcare Act (2017)

1. Telemedicine & Prescription Protocols (NMC Guidelines 2020)

In accordance with the National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines:

  • Prescription Restrictions: As mandated by Indian law, Schedule X medications and controlled habit-forming narcotics will not be prescribed via teleconsultation. Any requirement for restricted medications mandates an in-person clinical visit.
  • Emergency Limitation: Telepsychiatry is not a substitute for hospital emergency care. In the event of acute medical emergencies, severe psychosis, self-harm ideation, or active suicidal crises, the patient or caregiver must immediately approach the nearest emergency medical facility or contact the national emergency services (112 or Tele-MANAS at 14416).
  • Patient Identity Verification: Patients are required to provide verifiable identification (Name, Age, Address, and Valid Phone/Email) prior to the issuance of any medical advice or prescription.

2. Privacy Policy & Confidentiality (DPDP Act)

Your privacy and clinical documentation are governed under strict medical ethics and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework:

  • Confidentiality: Clinical discussions, history, and medical records remain strictly confidential between the patient and the treating psychiatrist. Records are never shared or sold to third parties without prior informed consent.
  • Exceptions to Confidentiality: Disclosure without consent occurs strictly when required by law or where there is an imminent, verifiable danger to the life of the patient or others.
  • Payment Security: Payment transactions are executed directly via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). No banking passwords, UPI PINs, or credit/debit card numbers are stored on our servers.

3. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

To respect professional clinical time and appointment availability:

  • Rescheduling: Sessions can be rescheduled up to 12 hours prior to the scheduled appointment slot at no additional charge.
  • Cancellation & Refund: Cancellations made more than 24 hours prior to the appointment are eligible for a 100% refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours or patient no-shows will not be refunded.
  • Doctor Unavailability: In the rare event that the treating physician cannot attend due to an emergency, the patient will receive the option of a full refund or an expedited priority rescheduling slot.
  • Settlement Timeline: Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment source (UPI/Bank/Card) within 5–7 working days as per standard banking procedures.
